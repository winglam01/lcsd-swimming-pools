How to read this page

LCSD operates a two-tier calendar: a weekly cleansing closure (the routine “day off”, normally from 10:00 a.m. to the end of the second session, moved to another day in the same week if it lands on a public holiday) and an annual maintenance closure that takes the indoor (and/or outdoor) pools out of service. Both are listed per pool below.

Reading the dates. “Closes → Reopens” is the indoor pool’s annual maintenance window: the date the indoor pool stops opening, and the date it opens again. “Indoor open period” is the complementary part of the year. Where a complex has both indoor and outdoor pools, the outdoor window is shown as well, because a closed outdoor pool often means “indoor only” even though the indoor pool itself is open.
